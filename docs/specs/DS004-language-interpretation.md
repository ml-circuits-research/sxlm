## Introduction

Language interpretation gives a reasoning caller a declared semantic representation of the supplied English. Its responsibility is to preserve what was interpreted and expose what was not understood. Formal inference cannot repair a missing or ambiguous interpretation by guessing an answer.

## Core Content

### Grammar authority and extension

Installed [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) and lexical knowledge must be reconstructed by [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) [providers](../wiki.html#definition-provider). An active [pack](../wiki.html#definition-pack) must not carry a raw `grammar` field. Offline `compileGrammarKnowledge` may compile an authored proposal but must label it compiled, not induced. `induceConstruction` must preserve its separate anti-unification derivation before compiling the proposal.

The [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) merger must preserve [provider](../wiki.html#definition-provider) order and reject incompatible collisions. An incremental extension must not replace the start category or segmentation policy, or redefine a lexical class incompatibly. A new semantic construction should compose in an existing category. Productions must invoke typed semantic [circuits](../wiki.html#definition-circuit) over child meanings. Shared subject bindings, existential referents, argument order, explicit polarity and attributed [context](../wiki.html#definition-context) must survive nested composition.

The finite English [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) does not define arbitrary pronoun resolution, passive paraphrase, generalized tense or unrestricted quantification. An unsupported construction must produce a [coverage](../wiki.html#definition-coverage) gap or explicit refusal. A negative instance must not be treated as the negation of an existential assertion.

`compilePhraseKnowledge` may compile explicitly supplied `{category, surface, value}` observations into shared-prefix [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) and [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) lexical constructors. It must use the parent tokenizer, preserve original terminal spellings for the installed normalization policy, retain shorter phrases alongside their extensions, and preserve ambiguous constant meanings, record order and signed zero. Generated semantic actions must project child values; they must not select answers or recognize domain words in native code. This compiler accepts constant lexical meanings only. Scope-dependent meanings and variable composition belong in ordinary semantic [circuits](../wiki.html#definition-circuit). Its artifacts are authored lexical supervision, not [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) induction. Production and execution bounds remain unchanged.

The optional elementary [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) exposes `ElementaryRelationNoun` for supplied binary relation meanings. Plain and article-bearing `of` constructions must compose through the existing `Prep`, `Comp` and `VP` categories, preserving argument order, negation, restrictions and rule bindings. Comparative words and directions must supply their predicates through lexical data. Subject and object selection must retain distinct variable positions; a new relation meaning must not require a whole-question production. Singular/plural count-noun entries are curated lexical supervision, not a productive morphology learner.

A learned predicate must compose through existing surrounding categories when their meanings apply. The passive construction [pack](../wiki.html#definition-pack) supplies a `Comp` production for a participle and its agent phrase. Existing copula, negation, assertion, query and explanation [circuits](../wiki.html#definition-circuit) consume that predicate. There must be no separate sentence or passive-question answer table. Its learned action preserves the agent phrase's full constraints and demonstrated argument order; lexical meanings and the surrounding [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) remain explicitly supplied knowledge. The training and transfer obligations are defined in [DS008](DS008-learning-and-promotion.md) and [DS011](DS011-evaluation-and-evidence.md).

### Category language and instance references

Generic classification must use the same `kind` relation as category definitions. The shared `knowledge.concepts` [provider](../wiki.html#definition-provider) must declare category identities and their supplied singular/plural forms. A singular copular question with `a` or `an` must use category semantics only when its subject and every unary complement are declared categories. The decision must depend on lexical category declarations, not on which interpretation yields a preferred answer. `some` must retain existential semantics and require an actual witness. Named noun phrases, relational complements and undeclared categories retain their existing interpretation. This convention resolves a bounded generic reading; it does not infer all English quantifier meanings.

Plural category questions must compose through `ConceptPlural`, `Comp` and the existing conjunction and negation operations. Unsupported property or relational complements must become interpretation gaps. Supplied plural forms must remain lexical observations; the host must not guess inflections. A category description, its singular/plural question variants and a classification question must preserve the same category identifiers. The model must not add an instance merely to answer a generic question.

Single-token phrase meanings must also populate their destination lexical index. A supplied alias must not acquire a second identity meaning from an open-class fallback. Multiword phrases must preserve their composed category and the parent token policy. Distinct supplied meanings remain ambiguous.

Category descriptions may verbalize asserted classification edges present in the returned proof [certificate](../wiki.html#definition-certificate). Classification explanations must identify those supporting statements, without presenting taxonomic entailment as a biological or causal explanation. Explicit negative support, conflicts, unknown answers and interpretation gaps must retain their respective outcomes. Concept and instance identifiers share the formal term space; teachers must avoid identity collisions. A general higher-order ontology and unrestricted descriptive generation are outside this profile.

The everyday question family must use concept-domain references for supplied plurals and the declared generic article convention, and instance-domain references for names and existential restrictions. The supplied tails “used for,” “help us do” and “usually need” carry relation and realization data. A separate learned plural-verb construction must reuse the existing verb category. Ordinary individual verb and preposition questions retain their existing parse; identical surface forms must not gain incompatible alternatives merely because reified property knowledge is installed. Bare mass-noun meanings and “made of” are supplied lexical observations, not host substitutions or morphological inference.

Property answer wording may use supplied category forms and relation clauses when the reference is unrestricted and the result is answered without gaps. Restricted existential answers retain their ordinary selection wording. Qualified answers must retain their qualifier. Description proof statements are displayed in reverse certificate order to begin nearer the queried classification; this ordering is presentation, not an additional fact or causal explanation.

### Lexical callback contracts

`Grammar` must require explicit pure [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) programs through `policy.prepare`, `policy.tokenize` and `policy.terminal`. Preparation accepts `{with: object}` and returns an object. Tokenization accepts `{text: string}` and returns an array. Terminal matching accepts `{symbol: object, token: object, knowledge: object}` and returns an array of semantic values. Missing, impure or incompatible programs must fail installation. There is no native terminal fallback.

[SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) policy must own lexical index construction, literal and category lookup, class exclusion, case sensitivity and returned surface preservation. Index identity must avoid delimiter collisions. Alternative values must preserve distinctions observable by subsequent [circuits](../wiki.html#definition-circuit), including record order and signed zero. Installation must charge lexical preparation, and parser callbacks must share request [budgets](../wiki.html#definition-budget) and traces.

The [normalization method](../wiki.html#definition-normalization-method) must remain distinct from the surrounding authored lexical policies. Its two-operation Unicode composition is synthesized from eight teacher observations and an explicit pure library. Reproduction must match deployed source and runtime-bound evidence. The method must be reused for literal spellings, lexical entries and parser token values. Sixteen held-out normalization pairs and [ablation](../wiki.html#definition-ablation) provide scoped evidence, not learned token boundaries.

### Recognition and source fidelity

Token records must identify exact, ordered, non-overlapping half-open UTF-16 [source spans](../wiki.html#definition-source-span). Their `text` must equal the source slice and each span must have positive length. Invalid, overlapping or forged spans must fail. Semantic evidence must retain original source offsets even when normalized token values differ in length.

The recognizer may support left-recursive category composition. It must retain distinct complete meanings and report ambiguity rather than selecting a meaning because its answer is convenient. Pure terminal memoization must include the full symbol and token identity, including offsets, and charge cache hits. Disabling caching must preserve observable results.

Document policy must segment the input, invoke recognition, validate meanings and lower successful interpretations through [circuits](../wiki.html#definition-circuit). Unparsed, ambiguous and invalid meanings must remain visible as gaps. An ordinary lowering failure may become a gap; resource exhaustion must propagate. Unsupported questions must not mutate [session](../wiki.html#definition-session) facts.

### Segmentation policy

`text.segment` must accept `{text: string, boundaries: array, keep: array}` and return an array of segment records through six authored [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) modules. It must close a segment at a supplied boundary token or the final token, preserve only requested delimiters in its text, skip leading whitespace and omit empty segments. Its `start` identifies the first retained character. Its `end` identifies the end of the consumed final token, including a discarded delimiter. The `text` field must omit trailing whitespace. Consequently, a segment's consumed source interval may include a discarded delimiter and surrounding whitespace; token records retain the stricter exact-slice contract above.

[Document interpretation](../wiki.html#definition-document-interpretation), summaries, completion and offline [sequence learning](../wiki.html#definition-sequence-learning) must use this explicit segmentation program. `kernel.text.segment` must not be installed. The general `kernel.text.trimStart` operation may remove the maximal leading ECMAScript WhiteSpace/LineTerminator prefix; it must preserve remaining UTF-16 code units and charge work by input length. It must not choose boundaries or recognize sentences.

`Grammar.tokenize` must execute the parser's installed pure token policy and validate returned [source spans](../wiki.html#definition-source-span). Both recognition and construction alignment must use this method. A construction learner must not bypass the model's policy through the legacy global native tokenizer. DS008 defines the separate captured sequence-policy contract. The six segmentation programs are authored; their migration must not acquire learning credit.

### Native boundary and validation

Native token boundaries, word [extraction](../wiki.html#definition-extraction) and Earley scheduling are unreviewed policy paths under DS000. The parser's [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) token policy replaces values produced from native spans; it does not remove the native tokenizer. Summary word [extraction](../wiki.html#definition-extraction) uses the native path. Completion and segmentation use `lexical.tokenize`, including its learned value normalization. The general Unicode operations accept explicit normalization and case modes. Restricted character membership must match the entire string and reject arbitrary regular-expression programs.

`test/lexical.test.mjs` covers callback contracts, historical comparison, source-span rejection, alternatives, Unicode transfer, 3,905 finite character-membership comparisons and [ablation](../wiki.html#definition-ablation). `test/document.test.mjs` covers document policy and [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) extension. `test/text-policy.test.mjs` checks 736 segmentation combinations against a frozen historical reference, explicit resource failures, captured policy contracts and learning under changed normalization and ignored-symbol policy. Those tests must not be reported as blind general-English evaluation.

### Lexical policy and Unicode mechanisms

The chart recognizer receives three explicit pure [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) entrypoints: lexical `prepare`, `tokenize` and `terminal`. Preparation accepts `{with: grammar}` and returns an immutable lexical index and classes. Tokenization accepts `{text}` and returns token records. Terminal matching accepts `{symbol, token, knowledge}` and returns an array of semantic values. The standalone `Grammar` constructor requires these entrypoints through its `policy` option; it has no native terminal fallback. `SymbolicModel` binds the corresponding [pack](../wiki.html#definition-pack) entrypoints and includes preparation work in installation metrics.

Seventeen authored [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) policies implement index construction, literal and lexical lookup, class selection, category exclusion, case sensitivity and surface preservation. The index is reconstructed by [circuits](../wiki.html#definition-circuit) from [circuit](../wiki.html#definition-circuit)-produced [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) knowledge. Its keys encode category and normalized spelling without delimiter collisions. Ambiguous lexical values preserve insertion order, record property order and signed zero. This preparation is not a new side table of authored answers.

`learned.lexical.normalize` is separately synthesized from eight typed examples in `training/lexical-normalization.sop`, using a teacher-supplied library and candidate operation names. The selected two-operation program composes compatibility normalization with lower casing. Examples distinguish this ordering from lower casing followed by normalization. Both the library choice and examples are author supervision; synthesis is not unsupervised language discovery. The same induced procedure processes literal terminals, [lexicon](../wiki.html#definition-lexicon) surfaces and parser token values. `build:lexical` reproduces its source and runtime-bound [receipt](../wiki.html#definition-receipt). The [bootstrap](../wiki.html#definition-bootstrap) stores its evidence under `training.components.lexicalNormalization`.

The host's new operations are string normalization with an explicit Unicode form, case conversion with an explicit operation, and restricted character-class membership. They contain no words, answer templates or [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar)-category names. Membership accepts concatenated character classes with at most one variable repetition; arbitrary regular-expression programs are rejected. It checks the entire string, including any trailing newline. Unicode/ICU versions are already part of runtime identity. These are scoped computational contracts, not a certification of all Unicode implementations or the full [native closure](../wiki.html#definition-native-closure).

`Grammar.tokenize(text, options)` executes the installed token policy and validates its returned evidence. Recognition and [construction induction](../wiki.html#definition-construction-induction) use that same method. The parser validates token text against exact, ordered, non-overlapping half-open UTF-16 [source spans](../wiki.html#definition-source-span). Pure terminal results can be memoized within one parse by the complete symbol and token values, including property order and offsets. Each memo hit charges `terminalCacheHits`; disabling caching executes the policy again. [Grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) knowledge and selected programs are immutable for that parser. Semantic and lexical [circuit](../wiki.html#definition-circuit) calls share the outer execution trace and resource [budget](../wiki.html#definition-budget), so formerly hidden work is inspectable.

The existing 19-node proof regression consumes approximately 91,734 [VM](../wiki.html#definition-vm) nodes with caching and 92,800 without, versus roughly 25,000 before terminal policy migration. This motivated a general default [budget](../wiki.html#definition-budget) of 100,000 nodes. Explicit tighter limits stop computation without committing; the 3,000-node proof limit remains. This is additional visible computation, not an improved asymptotic algorithm or language-quality gain. Structured CLI results also contain fuller traces; consumers must allow for their size.

`test/lexical.test.mjs` checks primitive errors/[budgets](../wiki.html#definition-budget), 3,905 character-membership comparisons against independently written finite predicates, historical lexical/parser agreement, classes, exclusion, Unicode values, invalid [source spans](../wiki.html#definition-source-span), explicit callback contracts, and learning [ablation](../wiki.html#definition-ablation)/restoration. Sixteen held-out literal normalization pairs include compatibility symbols, combining forms and contextual casing. They are evaluator-owned and excluded from the learner's packet, but share the project author; they are not external blind data. Altering the induced module or removing its evidence invalidates derivation credit.

Native token boundaries remain: the tokenizer groups Unicode letters/numbers and certain internal connectors. Summary word [extraction](../wiki.html#definition-extraction) calls the native path, including its normalization convention. Segmentation and completion call `lexical.tokenize`, whose values use the learned normalization [circuit](../wiki.html#definition-circuit). The [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) parser overwrites token values through the learned normalization [circuit](../wiki.html#definition-circuit), but has not learned token boundaries. Earley agenda scheduling and semantic-alternative enumeration also remain native. These gaps stay visible in the vision audit; no claim is made that all lexical policy or the complete model is learned.

Failure realization receives a small status envelope. The potentially large execution trace is attached to the returned diagnostic result afterwards, preserving observability without consuming the failure renderer's [budget](../wiki.html#definition-budget) on irrelevant input hashing. A regression exercises resource failure after more than 20,000 traced nodes.

#### Document terminology

The offline phrase compiler accepts named terms and constant category meanings. It shares token prefixes and groups terminal leaves into lexical categories, then emits ordinary [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) [providers](../wiki.html#definition-provider) and [SOP](../wiki.html#definition-sop) projection actions. A document author can therefore supply multiword terms without adding a separate top-level production and semantic action for every spelling. The model still executes the installed lexical and recognition policies. This representation reduces measured chart work for the 320-term fixture in `test/phrase-knowledge.test.mjs`; it does not establish unrestricted document capacity. Differential checks retain ambiguity, shorter-prefix meanings, normalization and exact nested values, while a separate composition combines two supplied terms in a new structure.
The elementary extension also accepts a lexical relation noun once and composes it in assertions, relative restrictions, conditional rules and both directions of selection. For example, `mentor` in the relational-noun category supplies the same predicate as the existing verb. A supplied `custodian` meaning transfers to a universal rule and a selection question without editing the host or adding another sentence pattern. These checks establish the scope of the construction; they do not establish unrestricted noun-phrase interpretation or automatic morphology.
#### Coherent category questions

A declared category connects singular and plural descriptions to classification through the same relation. With the elementary knowledge, `What is a cat?`, `What are cats?` and `Tell me about cats.` return the supported classification chain. `Is a cat an animal?` checks category inclusion. `Is some cat an animal?` asks for an instance and remains unknown until the [context](../wiki.html#definition-context) supplies one. These meanings are selected by declared noun categories and determiners, with no answer-dependent interpretation choice.

The concept compiler accepts category identities, actual singular/plural forms and source-attributed inclusions or explicit disjointness. Multiword terms use the phrase compiler. Each concept declaration supplies reusable instance rules and lexical meanings; it does not supply question/answer pairs. The model can compose those meanings with existing relative restrictions and rule bindings. Classification descriptions verbalize supporting proof statements. They do not supply features absent from the source or explain biological causes.
#### Composing a learned predicate

The optional construction [pack](../wiki.html#definition-pack) learns the predicate “owned by Mira” in the existing `Comp` category. Existing [grammar](../wiki.html#definition-grammar) then uses it in statements, questions, explicit negation, explanations and supported rule contexts. Teaching it at that shared category makes “Is Pico owned by Mira?” use the same learned argument mapping as “Pico is owned by Mira.” The participle meanings remain supplied lexical knowledge; the composition procedure is learned from annotated phrases under the contract in [Training](../training.html).
#### Questions about properties

The everyday extension supports questions such as “What is a pencil used for?”, “What do plants need?” and “What do plants usually need?”. Answers distinguish supplied universal knowledge from typical features. Named objects and restricted existential phrases preserve their own identities and evidence requirements. The two question procedures are induced; lexical meanings and the convention for generic references are supplied. Individual questions such as “What does Mira need?” and “What is Mira made of?” continue to use the existing relational constructions.
